# KGP Feedback

One-click helper for the IIT Kharagpur ERP end-term feedback form (needed for the admit card).

**Install page:** https://kira-light-777.github.io/FEEDBACK-AUTOFILL/

- Drag the bookmark to your bookmarks bar, open ERP, click it.
- It opens the feedback form, goes through each course and teacher, and fills the answers.
- You type each captcha and decide per teacher whether to submit. At the end it opens your admit card.
- Runs only in your own browser, on your own logged-in ERP page. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
